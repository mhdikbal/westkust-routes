# Ethical Modelling of the Mining Enclave

## Core rule

People subjected to slavery, coercion, exile, punishment, or colonial classification are historical persons, not production resources.

## Representation requirements

- retain the source category;
- state that the category was imposed by the archival administration;
- preserve aggregate counts without inventing identities;
- represent coercion, supervision, restricted movement, food allowance, health, and forced mobility;
- avoid productivity rankings of human groups;
- avoid simulations that imply voluntary labour where coercion is documented.

## Counterfactual limits

Allowed:

- testing whether a recorded workflow is internally feasible;
- estimating uncertainty in tool or location capacity;
- comparing alternative topological reconstructions.

Not allowed as a default research output:

- optimizing extraction by reallocating enslaved people;
- ranking coerced groups by productivity;
- treating mortality or illness as mere throughput loss.

## Language

Use `people subjected to slavery` in analytical prose while preserving `slaven`, `slavinnen`, and `lijfeijgenen` in source fields.
