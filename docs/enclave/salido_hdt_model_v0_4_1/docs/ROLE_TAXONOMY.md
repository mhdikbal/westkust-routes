# Role Taxonomy

## Role hierarchy

Roles are non-exclusive. A person can hold an office, perform an activity, and serve as a commissioner in the same period.

### Administration

- `R-BERGHSCHRIJVER`: **Berghschrijver**, juru tulis tambang
- `R-BOOKKEEPER`: **Boekhouder**, pemegang buku

### CoercedLabourHierarchy

- `R-MANDOOR`: **Mandoor**, pengawas kelompok kerja paksa
- `R-MANDORESS`: **Mandores**, pengawas perempuan dalam kelompok kerja paksa
- `R-VOORSLAGER`: **Voorslager**, fungsi kerja historis; definisi belum final

### Craft

- `R-BAAS-SMIT`: **Baas Smit**, kepala pandai besi
- `R-SMIT`: **Smit**, pandai besi
- `R-TIMMERMAN`: **Timmerman**, tukang kayu
- `R-WAGENMAKER`: **Wagenmaker**, pembuat kereta
- `R-COOLBRANDER`: **Coolbrander**, pembuat arang

### Governance

- `R-COMMANDEUR`: **Commandeur**, kepala pemerintahan regional VOC

### Logistics

- `R-VOERMAN`: **Voerman**, kusir atau pengangkut
- `R-PACKHUIJSKNEGT`: **Packhuijsknegt**, pembantu gudang

### Medical

- `R-SIECKENVADER`: **Sieckenvader**, pengelola perawatan orang sakit
- `R-OPPERMEESTER`: **Oppermeester**, petugas medis senior
- `R-JONGEN-CHIRURGIJN`: **Jongen Chirurgijn**, asisten ahli bedah

### Military

- `R-RUIJTER`: **Ruijter**, prajurit berkuda

### Production

- `R-BERGWERKER`: **Bergwerker**, pekerja tambang

### TechnicalMining

- `R-BERGHOOPTMAN`: **Berghooftman**, kepala teknis pertambangan
- `R-MARKSCHEIDER`: **Marckscheijder**, ahli ukur dan orientasi tambang
- `R-ASSAIJEUR`: **Assaijeur**, penguji kadar emas dan perak
- `R-OPPERSTEIJGER`: **Oppersteijger**, pengawas senior bawah tanah

## Inheritance

A role family supplies default skills but does not create facts. For example, `Assaijeur` implies compatibility with assay work, but a specific assay event still requires documentary evidence or a reconstructed assignment.

## Ditto expansion

Where `d:o` means the role repeated from the preceding entry, the expanded role must retain a note that the role was inherited from list structure.
